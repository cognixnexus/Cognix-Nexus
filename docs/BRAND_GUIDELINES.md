# Cognix Brand Guidelines

## Brand relationship

- **Cognix Core** is the umbrella vision and intelligence foundation.
- **Cognix Nexus** is the product/platform built around that vision.
- Use the names consistently. Do not describe Cognix Nexus as a separate, unrelated product.

## Visual direction

The visual language is calm, technical, and knowledge-oriented: deep navy foundations, restrained cyan highlights, and violet accents. Avoid noisy gradients, excessive neon, generic robot imagery, or decorative elements that compete with the product message.

## Core palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Space | `#080D1B` | Primary dark background |
| Midnight Blue | `#101A35` | Secondary background |
| Deep Violet | `#17102F` | Accent background |
| Cognix Cyan | `#61E7D7` | Primary highlight |
| Cognix Violet | `#8B7CFF` | Secondary highlight |
| Soft White | `#F4F7FF` | Main text |
| Muted Blue Grey | `#9EACC9` | Supporting text |

## Typography

Use clean sans-serif typography such as Inter, Segoe UI, or system sans-serif. Prefer concise headings, clear hierarchy, and generous spacing. Avoid all-caps body copy; reserve uppercase tracking for small labels.

## Logo and banner assets

- `assets/cognix-core-banner.svg` is the shared 1280 × 420 README/banner artwork.
- `apps/web-platform/frontend/public/logo.svg` is the current application logo source.
- Preserve aspect ratio and clear space. Do not stretch, recolor, or place the mark on low-contrast backgrounds.
- The SVG banner is suitable for repository documentation. GitHub's separate social-preview setting is configured in repository settings and is not changed by adding a README image.

## Voice and product language

Preferred themes:
- Connected intelligence
- Evidence-aware knowledge
- Research and recall
- Connected thinking
- Useful context

Prefer accurate capability descriptions over absolute claims. Clearly distinguish current implementation, planned direction, and experimental integrations.

## Ownership

Keep this guide and the banner in sync when the brand system changes.
